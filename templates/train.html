What do these numbers mean?

R² (coefficient of determination) — how much of the variation in admission chances the model explains. 1.0 is perfect; 0 means no better than always guessing the average.

MAE (mean absolute error) — the average size of a prediction mistake. An MAE of 0.05 means predictions are off by about 5 percentage points on average.

RMSE (root mean squared error) — like MAE, but large mistakes count more. If RMSE is much bigger than MAE, a few predictions are badly wrong.

Compare training with testing: testing scores come from students the model never saw, so they show how it will behave on new applicants.
