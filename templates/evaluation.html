C. Feature importance Q2

Permutation importance shuffles one feature at a time in the held-out test set and measures how much the test MAE gets worse. A bigger increase means the model depends on that feature more.

Academic features: GRE, TOEFL, CGPA, Research.
Reviewer-influenced features: SOP, LOR, University Rating.

Importance measures predictive dependence only. A feature can be highly predictive and still encode past reviewer bias — high importance is not evidence of fairness.
